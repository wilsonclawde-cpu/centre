Why locate here

A professional home, with a bit of history.

Kesteven Business Centre is set in Sleaford's Old Police Station — a characterful, recently refurbished building full of history, opposite the council offices and a short walk from the town centre and rail station. Independent businesses, together under one roof.

Sleaford sits at the crossroads of Lincolnshire: a short drive from Grantham, Lincoln and Newark-on-Trent, with Boston, Bourne, Spalding and Stamford within easy reach and the A15 and A17 on the doorstep. For a business drawing staff or clients from across North Kesteven — Ruskington, Heckington, Billinghay, Metheringham, Navenby, Ancaster — it is a base that is easy for everyone to get to, with parking directly behind.

Simple licences, one all-inclusive monthly invoice, and none of the long-lease commitment. Tenants have 24-hour access, so you can work the hours that suit your business. Move in, get to work, and grow at your own pace — with GLOW café downstairs when you need a break.

Good to know

Office space in Sleaford — your questions.

How much does office space cost at Kesteven Business Centre?

Serviced office suites in Sleaford start from £250 per month, inclusive of heating and lighting. Sizes range from 113 sq ft up to 964 sq ft, and there is secure cell storage from £100 per month.

Are the offices serviced and all-inclusive?

Yes. Every suite is let on a simple monthly licence covering heating and lighting, on flexible terms with no long lease to tie you down.

Can I access my office out of hours?

Yes. Tenants have 24-hour access to Kesteven Business Centre, seven days a week, so you can come and go whenever it suits your business — early starts, late finishes and weekends included.

What size offices are available in Sleaford?

From compact one-person suites of around 113 sq ft up to 964 sq ft, and connected suites can be let together. Whatever stage your business is at, there is a space to suit.

Where is Kesteven Business Centre?

Kesteven Street, Sleaford, Lincolnshire, NG34 7DT — the town’s former police station, a short walk from the market place and Sleaford railway station, with parking directly behind.

Can I rent an office month to month?

Yes. Terms are flexible with short notice periods, so you can scale up or down as your business grows.

What else is on site?

GLOW Sleaford — a superfood café serving açaí bowls, protein shakes, smoothies, coffee and matcha — is on the ground floor, with the rest of Sleaford town centre on the doorstep.
