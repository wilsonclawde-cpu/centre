Good to know

Meeting room hire in Sleaford — your questions.

How much does it cost to hire the meeting room?

£15 per hour, £50 for a half day or £90 for a full day, with tea and coffee included. Catering by GLOW Sleaford is priced on request per booking.

How many people does the meeting room seat?

The meeting room at Kesteven Business Centre seats up to 12 people.

Can I book the meeting room by the hour?

Yes. Book by the hour at £15 an hour, or take a half day for £50 or a full day for £90 if you need longer.

Is catering available?

Yes. GLOW Sleaford, the superfood café on the ground floor of the building, can cater your booking — açaí bowls, protein shakes, smoothies, coffee and matcha, protein salads and bakes. Catering is priced on request per booking, so tell us what you would like when you book. Tea and coffee are included in every booking.

Where do I park?

There is a car park directly behind the building. Kesteven Business Centre is on Kesteven Street, Sleaford NG34 7DT, in the town centre and a short walk from the market place and Sleaford railway station.
